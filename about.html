Our approach

Real infrastructure, not a login screen

We install real infrastructure, not a subscription with a login screen. Every system we deploy runs primarily on open-source software — Home Assistant, Nextcloud, Immich, Jellyfin, Frigate — so it keeps working whether or not the company that made it is still around, still supported, or still free next year.

Where a closed-source option is genuinely the better fit, we say so and explain the trade-off, rather than quietly picking whatever's easiest to sell.

Every engagement includes a plain-language walkthrough, clear documentation of what was installed and how it's configured, and pricing that's fixed up front for standard tiers — no hourly surprises. We serve the Tri-Cities region of East Tennessee in person, with custom software engagements available remotely nationwide.
