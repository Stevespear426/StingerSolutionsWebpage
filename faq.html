Getting started

What does "self-hosted" actually mean?

Instead of your files, photos, or camera footage living on a company's servers that you pay a monthly fee to access, they live on a small computer in your own home that you own outright. You get an app or a web page that looks and works like the commercial version — it's just running on your hardware, on your network, under your control.

Do I need to be tech-savvy to use this day-to-day?

No. Day-to-day, it looks like any other app — you open Nextcloud instead of Dropbox, Immich instead of Google Photos. The technical part is our job during install; every system ships with a plain-language walkthrough so your whole household knows how to use it before we leave.

I don't know which tier or setup I need — what now?

Tell us about your household — how many people, how many phones, whether you want cameras, how far back you want footage retained — and we'll recommend a tier. There's no obligation, and most conversations start with us asking questions, not pitching a package.

Cost & process

How much does this actually cost?

Home server systems start at $1,400 (Starter) and go up to $4,500 (Prosumer) depending on household size and use. Camera systems start at $450. Every price includes hardware, professional install, and a walkthrough — not just a box on your doorstep. See Home Server Systems and Camera & Security for full tier breakdowns.

Are there any monthly fees?

Not required. The hardware is a one-time purchase and it's yours. The only optional recurring cost is a maintenance plan ($30–75/mo) if you'd rather we handle updates and backup checks instead of doing it yourself.

What's included in the price?

Shop pre-configuration, on-site installation, network integration, device pairing (Zigbee, Z-Wave, Matter), and a walkthrough. You're not left with a pile of hardware and a manual.

How long does installation take?

Most home server installs are done in a single day on-site, after shop pre-configuration beforehand. Camera installs vary by cable runs and camera count — we'll give you a specific estimate once we know your layout.

Can I start small and add on later?

Yes. Most households start with a Starter or Standard home server and add cameras, storage, or the external-access add-on later. The systems are built to expand rather than get replaced.

How it works

What happens if my internet goes down?

Everything on your local network keeps working — files, photos, your smart home, and camera recording all run without the internet. You'd only lose remote access (checking cameras from work, for example) until it's back.

What happens if the power goes out?

Every system includes at least a basic UPS for a graceful shutdown, so you don't lose data mid-write. Standard and Prosumer tiers add longer battery runtime, and a grid-down resilience add-on (extended battery or solar) is available if you want the system to keep running through longer outages.

Can I access my files and cameras when I'm away from home?

Yes, through a VPN (Tailscale or WireGuard) set up during install — you connect back into your home network securely without exposing it to the open internet. If you'd rather not use a VPN app, we also offer a no-VPN external access option with real HTTPS certificates and additional hardening.

What happens if a hard drive fails?

The NAS in every tier uses redundant drives (RAID), so a single drive failure doesn't lose your data — you replace the drive and it rebuilds. We size and configure this during install so it's not something you have to think about.

Is my data actually private — even from Stinger Solutions?

Yes. Once it's installed, your system runs entirely on your network under accounts and credentials you control. We don't have standing access to your files, photos, or footage after the walkthrough — if you want us to help troubleshoot later, that's arranged with you directly, not something we can do silently.

Cameras & custom software

Does any camera footage touch the cloud?

No. We run Frigate, a fully local NVR with AI object detection, on the same box as the rest of your system. Footage is recorded, processed, and stored on your own hardware — nothing is uploaded anywhere by default.

I have an app idea but no technical spec — can you still help?

That's the normal starting point. Custom software engagements start as a conversation about what you actually need, not a package you pick off a menu — see Custom Software Development for how that process works.

Do you build for both iPhone and Android?

Yes — native iOS, Android, and desktop apps from a single cross-platform codebase, so you're not paying to build and maintain the same app twice.
