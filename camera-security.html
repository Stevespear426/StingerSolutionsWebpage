Our hardware philosophy

Software: Frigate — open-source, fully local NVR with AI object detection. No cloud, no subscription, runs on the same mini PC as the rest of your system.

Cameras: PoE only, standardized on Reolink or Amcrest — one cable per camera for power and data. We don't install WiFi cameras as standard; the streams are unreliable and drop.

Retention: configurable per camera — continuous footage for days, event clips for weeks or months. We'll ask: "how far back do you want to be able to look?" and size your storage to match.

Doorbells: PoE doorbells don't ring a traditional chime — you'll get an app notification, or we can add an optional $30–35 chime accessory.

Alternate option: self-hosted UniFi Protect

If you already have UniFi networking gear and want a polished, all-in-one native app, we can build on self-hosted UniFi Protect instead of Frigate.
