How a custom project runs

  1. 01
    Discovery call

    We talk through what you need, how you'll use it, and what "done" looks like.

  2. 02
    Scoped proposal

    A fixed quote and timeline, built around your actual requirements — not a template.

  3. 03
    Build & check-ins

    Regular progress updates so there are no surprises at delivery.

  4. 04
    Handoff & support
